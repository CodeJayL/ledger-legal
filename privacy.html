This Privacy Policy explains how Ledger (“Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our AI-powered automation system and related software (the “Software”).

By installing or using the Software, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

1. Overview

Ledger is designed primarily as a locally run automation and accounting tool. By default, data processed by the Software is stored on your own device or local infrastructure and is not automatically transmitted to Ledger-managed servers.

2. Information We Process

In the course of using the Software, the following categories of information may be processed:

2.1 Personal Information

  • Names, business names, and contact details that appear in your documents;
  • Account identifiers you configure within the Software.

2.2 Financial and Accounting Information

  • Invoices, receipts, statements, and other financial records you choose to import;
  • Accounting classifications, categories, and related metadata;
  • Transaction-level data that you process using the Software.

2.3 Technical Information (Local)

  • Configuration files and settings;
  • Local logs or diagnostic information created by the Software.

2.4 API Tokens and Credentials

  • Tokens and credentials for third-party accounting platforms (such as QuickBooks) that you choose to connect.

2.5 AI-Generated Content

  • Summaries, categorizations, recommendations, and other outputs generated by AI models based on your input data.

3. How We Use Information

The Software uses the information you provide in order to:

  • Automate data entry, categorization, and reconciliation tasks;
  • Generate AI-powered suggestions, summaries, and reports;
  • Facilitate integrations with third-party services such as QuickBooks;
  • Improve the quality and reliability of the local features of the Software.

Ledger does not sell or rent your information to third parties.

4. Local Storage

By default, all data processed by the Software is stored locally on your device or local environment. Ledger does not automatically receive copies of your financial records, personal information, or tokens.

You have full control over:

  • Which documents you import into the Software;
  • How long you retain them;
  • When and how you delete them.

5. Third-Party Services

When you choose to connect the Software to a third-party service such as QuickBooks, some data may be exchanged with that service based on your configuration and actions.

These third-party services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Ledger does not control how those services handle your data.

6. Security

We design the Software with security in mind, including options for local encryption of sensitive information such as API tokens. However, no system is completely secure.

You are responsible for:

  • Securing your device, operating system, and local network;
  • Managing access to your files and backups;
  • Protecting any credentials or tokens used with third-party services.

7. Data Retention

Because data is stored locally, retention periods are largely under your control. You may delete documents, logs, or configuration files at any time from your own systems.

Ledger does not maintain centralized backups of your local data.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, or delete it.

Since Ledger does not generally store your data on our own servers, many of these rights are exercised directly by you on your own device by managing, editing, or deleting your local files.

The Software is intended for use by adults and business users only and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
